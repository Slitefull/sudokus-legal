Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026

Sudokus ("the app") is made by Maksym Tytechko. This policy explains what data the app handles. Short version: we do not collect personal data, we do not track you, and there are no ads.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (unfinished games, solved levels, times, mistakes, hints) and your settings are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this data.

On iPhone, your solved puzzles can also sync between your own devices through your iCloud account (Apple's iCloud key-value storage). Only Apple and you can see this data; we cannot. You can turn it off in the app's Settings. A backup file you export from Settings goes wherever you choose to save or share it.

The daily reminder, if you turn it on, is a notification scheduled on your device. No push server is involved.

A challenge code you share contains only the puzzle's code and, if you choose, your time. It is sent by you, through the app you pick, to the people you pick.

Purchases

Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your payment details. To confirm and restore purchases, the app uses RevenueCat, Inc. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID generated on your device and the purchase receipt from Apple or Google. It does not receive your name, email or contact details. RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Updates

When the app starts, it checks in the background whether a newer version of its code is available, using EAS Update by Expo (650 Industries, Inc.). According to Expo, this request contains your device's operating system, the app's project ID and a random token for this installation. It contains no device identifiers and nothing about your games. Expo's privacy policy: https://expo.dev/privacy

What we do not do

No accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking across apps or websites, no sale of data.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights and contact

Because we hold no personal data, there is nothing for us to access, export or delete. To ask about the anonymous purchase record kept by RevenueCat, or for any question about this policy, write to 0xmaksym@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.

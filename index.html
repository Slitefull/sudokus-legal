Sudokus

Calm, ad-free classic sudoku for iPhone. Every puzzle has one solution and is graded by the techniques it needs.

Support

Questions, bugs or ideas: 0xmaksym@gmail.com. Please mention your iPhone model and iOS version.

Purchases

Sudokus Pro is a one-time purchase. To get it back on a new device, open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases. Refunds go through Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your progress

Progress stays on your device and, if you keep iCloud sync on, in your own iCloud. Settings > Progress exports a backup file you can import on another device.
